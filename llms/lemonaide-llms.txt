# Lemonaide

> Lemonaide is a Bangkok-headquartered B2B used-car and insurtech platform operating across Southeast Asia. It sells software and operated services to used-car dealerships and insurers, with the stated mission of reducing asymmetric information in the automotive market. Lemonaide is backed by 500 Global. As of 2026-07-19 Lemonaide publishes no public API, developer portal, or machine-readable specification.

## Products

- [Dealer Management System](https://lemonaide.co.th/lemonaide-saas/): Dealership operations in a single app — stock, leads, sales, vehicle tax, sourcing, SLA, financing, partners, documentation, and service management.
- [Dealer CRM System](https://lemonaide.co.th/lemonaide-dealer-crm-system/): Customer management with microtargeting.
- [Digital Escrow](https://lemonaide.co.th/lemonaide-digital-ledger/): Secure payment and settlement between transacting parties.
- [After-Sales BPO Service](https://lemonaide.co.th/aftersales-bpo-services/): Outsourced after-sales customer support.
- [Warranty TPA Service](https://lemonaide.co.th/warranty-tpa-services/): Third-party warranty claims administration for insurers.
- [Digital Roadside Service](https://lemonaide.co.th/digital-roadside-services/): 24-hour roadside assistance dispatch.
- [Vehicle Inspection Services](https://lemonaide.co.th/vehicles-authentication/): Vehicle condition inspection and authentication.
- [Audit Assessment Services](https://lemonaide.co.th/partners-authentication-services/): Audit and assessment of partner repair workshops.
